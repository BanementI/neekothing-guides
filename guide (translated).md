Black Market

The technology of "blue space" has made it possible to significantly simplify the transfer of information, cargo, and human resources over long distances. Professor Cherenkov, a distant descendant of Pavel Cherenkov, designed a device known as the "Cherenkov Bluespace Encryptor" or, in everyday life, the "bluespace encryptor". With its help, it became possible to communicate (albeit almost blindly) with the inhabitants of other galaxies and even metagalaxies. For the most part, [these inhabitants were either aggressive gangs of slave traders, or isolated, dying civilizations, or - non-human cultures that were reluctant to make contact, since they had already had time to drink tears and blood in the process of previous clashes with the sons and daughters of the human race.](https://forum.ss13.ru/index.php?showtopic=5790) The purchase of slaves seemed attractive to the Union, which was always in need of labor, but, unfortunately, it was not possible due to the weak development of the blue space technology of the slave traders, as well as due to the technical limitations of the "cipher" itself. The xenos flatly refused to contact people, and the Union did not want to enter into any relations with the semi-feral inhabitants of dying worlds. However, there were those who responded to the call from the black abyss of space. These people and non-people are representatives of the "cipher" subculture, using devices like Cherenkov's invention to trade drugs, weapons, valuable medicines and just all sorts of squabbles. Contacts with these secretive space hucksters and speculators significantly enriched the Union, but also led to the emergence of a vast black market, where anyone could buy both a harmless thing like a T-shirt or a bong, and a weapon or a drug prohibited from free circulation. 
1) "Cipherboxes" are scattered all over the station and the space around it, it's pretty easy to find one. They are semi-legal, but the cops and captain will probably turn a blind eye to your trading operations until you steal and sell something valuable (like a gun or an important circuit board). 
2) To buy something, click on the "cipherbox" and select a merchant from the list. Remember that even merchants with the same "specialization" (like prescription drugs, spice, or weapons) may have different assortments and prices. After purchasing, you will be told the address of the cache (like "Northwest Bar"). Go there and pick up the goods before someone steals them (access to the Maint may come in handy here - caches are often left there). The cache is initially disguised, but will appear when you stand on the cell with it. Right-click on the cipher > Look for hidden packages to reveal all the caches in the visible radius, making the process much easier. Hardcore drug addicts sometimes see invisible boxes too: beware of cognitologists!
3) To sell something, simply click on the "cipher" with the item. You will be told its price in Cherenkov (local money) and asked if you want to sell it. Supply cartridges, electronics (even the most common ones), weapons and some drugs are especially valuable, but many items are suitable for sale.
